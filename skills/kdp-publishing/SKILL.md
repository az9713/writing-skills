# KDP Publishing for a First-Time Author

Assume the author has never published anything, has no KDP account, no designer, and no formatting software. Walk them through one step at a time. The author does every step that needs their account, identity, or money; the AI explains, prepares text, and checks.

**Facts below were checked on the KDP Help Center on 2026-09-17.** KDP changes its rules. Before you tell the author a rule, price band, or percentage, check the linked page again if you can. If you cannot, say the date the fact was checked.

Start only when the manuscript is finished (book-writing §22 Definition of Done).

## Step 1 — Create the account (the author does this)

1. Sign in at kdp.amazon.com with an Amazon customer account, or click Sign up.
2. Enter author, payment (bank), and tax information. Amazon must validate the tax identity before any book can be published.
3. The tax questionnaire produces a US tax form (a W-8 form for authors outside the US). The default US withholding for non-US authors is 30%; a tax treaty may reduce it. The AI must not give tax advice: tell the author to ask a tax adviser if unsure.

Sources: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200620010 · https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201274750

## Step 2 — Answer the AI-content question honestly

KDP asks, at publishing time, whether the book contains AI-generated content.

- **AI-generated** (must be disclosed): text, images, or translations created by an AI tool — even if the author then edited them substantially. This includes AI-made cover and interior images.
- **AI-assisted** (no disclosure required): the author created the content and used AI to edit, refine, error-check, or brainstorm.

Help the author decide from how the book was actually made. A book drafted section by section by the AI with book-writing (§10 Phase H) contains AI-generated text, even after heavy editing. Do not advise the author to answer "no" to avoid the question. The author is responsible for the content meeting KDP's guidelines, including intellectual property rights.

Source: https://kdp.amazon.com/help?topicId=G200672390

## Step 3 — ISBN

- Kindle eBook: no ISBN needed.
- Paperback and hardcover: an ISBN is required. KDP offers one free; each format needs its own. The alternative is buying one from the national ISBN agency. The free KDP ISBN is simplest for a first book.

Source: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GTJ8LBXL6Z4WV5QX

## Step 4 — Format the interior

No design software is needed:

- KDP publishes free Microsoft Word manuscript templates for paperback and hardcover, with a formatting guide.
- Kindle Create is KDP's free formatting tool.

The AI can: convert the manuscript into the template's structure (headings, chapter starts, front matter: title page, copyright page, contents; back matter: about the author), and list what the author must check by eye in the preview. Record the final page count — the cover spine depends on it.

Sources: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834230 · https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GYVL2CASGU9ACFVU

## Step 5 — Cover

- KDP's free Cover Creator builds eBook, paperback, and hardcover covers from layouts and fonts, and places the ISBN barcode on the back.
- The AI writes the back-cover text from the positioning (book-writing §2A) and the author bio (§5). Every claim must be true of the book; every review quoted must be real.
- If an AI image tool makes cover art, that is AI-generated content for Step 2.

Source: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201113520

## Step 6 — Book details

The AI drafts, the author approves: title and subtitle (from book-writing §2A.5), the description (§23.2), author name, keywords, and categories. Keywords and categories must describe the book truthfully.

## Step 7 — Price

eBook, Amazon.com (checked 2026-09-17):

- 70% royalty option: list price $2.99–$12.99 (the upper limit rose from $9.99 on July 7, 2026). The 70% rate is paid on sales in eligible territories and minus a delivery cost (average $0.06 per unit, depends on file size).
- 35% royalty option: $0.99–$200.00 for files under 3 MB (higher minimums for larger files).

Paperback: royalty = (royalty rate × list price) − printing cost. On Amazon.com the rate is 50% at a list price of $9.98 or below and 60% at $9.99 or above; other marketplaces have their own thresholds. Printing cost depends on page count, ink type, and marketplace. KDP's royalty calculator shows the printing cost for a given page count.

Do not predict sales. State no sales figure that has not been checked.

Sources: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200634560 · https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200644210 · https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834330 · https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/royalty-calculator

## Step 8 — Preview, then publish

1. Upload the manuscript and cover. Open the online previewer and page through every page with the author.
2. Order a printed proof copy of the paperback before publishing, if the author can wait for it.
3. Publish. Tell the author that review by Amazon takes time and that the book is not live at once.

## Handing back

After each step, tell the author what is done, what is next, and the one sentence to type to continue ("Next publishing step").
